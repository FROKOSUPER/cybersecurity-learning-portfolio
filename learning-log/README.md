# Learning Log

This folder is for short, dated reflections on labs, courses, and projects. Each entry should focus on growth rather than simply listing completed tasks.

## Suggested Entry Template

```markdown
# YYYY-MM-DD — Topic

## What I Practiced

Briefly describe the lab or concept without sharing secrets or challenge answers.

## What I Learned

Explain the most important technical idea in your own words.

## A Problem I Solved

Describe an error, incorrect assumption, or obstacle and how you investigated it.

## Security Connection

Explain where this concept appears in cybersecurity work.

## Next Step

Choose one specific skill to practice next.
```

## Publishing Checklist

- Remove passwords, flags, tokens, IP addresses, and personal information
- Avoid complete walkthroughs for active challenges
- Verify that commands and explanations are accurate
- Use screenshots only after checking them for sensitive data
- Write in your own words and explain why the work matters
